### ⚡ E-Move — Electric Construction Material Trolley

<details open>
<summary><b>📂 Open project dossier</b></summary>

<br/>

**Concept:** A remote-controlled, battery-powered trolley designed to transport construction materials around a worksite.

**The problem:** Carrying materials manually over and over can require considerable effort and time.

**The idea:** Build a small, practical trolley prototype that can carry a basic payload and move under remote control.

**Planned engineering components:**

- 🛞 A compact trolley chassis and drive motors
- 🎮 Remote control using an RF transmitter and receiver
- 🔌 An Arduino-based control system
- 🔋 A suitable battery and motor-driver circuit
- 🧰 A practical, budget-conscious prototype

**What I want to learn from it:** motor control, circuit connections, embedded programming, power management, and mechanical integration.

**Project status:** Prototype concept and development journey.
